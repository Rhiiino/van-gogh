# Van Gogh — Assembly

Step-by-step build for **one** right barrel. Names match [catalog.md](catalog.md) and [bom.md](bom.md).

Each section opens with the parts that section uses. A printed part shows its catalog image there, at 48 px wide, next to the name. The steps use the name only.

<img src="catalog/barrel-middle.png" alt="barrel middle" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel middle**
**M3 heat-set insert**
**magnet**
**Ring bearing**

A. Press three **M3 heat-set inserts** into **barrel middle**.

B. Press six **magnets** into **barrel middle**.

C. Install the **Ring bearing** on top of **barrel middle**.

## 2. Preparing the Flywheel Base

<img src="catalog/flywheel-base.png" alt="flywheel base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel base**

## 3. Installing the Flywheels

<img src="catalog/flywheel.png" alt="flywheel" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel**
<img src="catalog/flywheel.png" alt="flywheel" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel**
<img src="catalog/flywheel-base.png" alt="flywheel base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel base**

A. Slip one **flywheel** onto each **Flywheel motor**.

B. Drive one **M5 set screw** into each **flywheel** until it tightens on the motor shaft and sits snug.

C. Set both assemblies on the **flywheel base** with the motor cord facing the rear of the base.

D. Route each motor's three wires through the wire channel to the underside of the **flywheel base**.

E. Secure each **Flywheel motor** to the **flywheel base** with two 6 mm **M3 bolts**. The bolts pass through the **flywheel base** and thread into the motor base.

## 4. Preparing the Power Housing

<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**

A. Press three **M3 heat-set inserts** into the three pillars in the **power housing**.

## 5. Preparing the Flywheel Carriage

<img src="catalog/flywheel-carriage.png" alt="flywheel carriage" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel carriage**
**magnet**

A. Press one **M3 heat-set insert** into the top of the **flywheel carriage** for the **magazine mount**.

B. Press two **M3 heat-set inserts** into the bottom of the **flywheel carriage** for the bolts through the **power housing** and the **flywheel base**.

C. Press two **magnets** into the **flywheel carriage**. One mates with the **barrel base L panel**. One mates with the **barrel base R panel**.

## 6. Installing the Power Housing

<img src="catalog/flywheel-base.png" alt="flywheel base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel base**
<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**

A. Align the underside of the **power housing** to the underside of the **flywheel base**. Make sure the wire slots align.

B. Route both motors' wires through the **power housing**.

C. Secure the **power housing** to the **flywheel base** with one 6 mm **M3 bolt**.

## 7. Preparing the Ammunition Guide

<img src="catalog/ammo-guide.png" alt="ammo guide" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **ammo guide**

A. Press one **M3 heat-set insert** into the **ammo guide**.

## 8. Installing the Ammunition Guide

<img src="catalog/flywheel-base.png" alt="flywheel base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel base**
<img src="catalog/ammo-guide.png" alt="ammo guide" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **ammo guide**

A. Insert the **ammo guide** into the slot on the **flywheel base**, with the extended leg sitting between the flywheels.

B. Secure the **flywheel base** to the **ammo guide** with one 12 mm **M3 bolt**. The bolt passes through the **flywheel base** and threads into the insert in the **ammo guide**.

## 9. Installing the Flywheel Carriage

<img src="catalog/flywheel-carriage.png" alt="flywheel carriage" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel carriage**
<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**
<img src="catalog/flywheel-base.png" alt="flywheel base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel base**

A. Place the **flywheel carriage** and align its heat-set insert holes with the holes on the **power housing** and the **flywheel base**.

B. Insert two 12 mm **M3 bolts** and secure all three together. Each bolt passes through the **power housing**, then the **flywheel base**, and threads into the **flywheel carriage**.

## 10. Installing ESCs

<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**
<img src="catalog/esc-casing.png" alt="ESC casing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **ESC casing**
<img src="catalog/esc-casing.png" alt="ESC casing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **ESC casing**
**ESC**

A. Route each motor's wires, where they come out of the **power housing**, around the pillar in front of that cutout.

B. Place each **ESC** into an **ESC casing**.

C. Seat both **ESC casing** parts in the **power housing**, one in each end slot. The three **ESC** prongs that will take the motor wires face the rear of the barrel, away from the **flywheel carriage** and toward the back of the **power housing**.

## 11. Preparing the Molex connector

**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**

A. Hold the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female** with the prong facing up. The six inlets run left to right. Seat a crimped lead in slots 1, 4, 5, and 6. Leave slots 2 and 3 empty.

- Slot 1 is power.
- Slot 4 is ground.
- Slot 5 is ESC 1.
- Slot 6 is ESC 2.

Slots 2 and 3 are the spare power and ground. Do not populate them.

B. Cut each of the four wires to about 45 mm. Strip both ends of each wire.

## 12. Preparing the positive line

**ESC**
**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**

A. Gather these three stripped ends and twist them together:

- The positive lead from each **ESC**.
- The lead from slot 1 of the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**.

B. Add solder to hold the twist.

This bundle is the positive line. It will later solder into the positive cup of the **XT90 male**.

## 13. Preparing the negative line

**ESC**
**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**

A. Gather these five stripped ends and twist them together:

- The negative power lead from each **ESC**.
- The negative signal lead from each **ESC**.
- The lead from slot 4 of the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**.

B. Add solder to hold the twist.

This bundle is the negative line. It will later solder into the negative cup of the **XT90 male**.

## 14. Assembling the power housing

<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**
**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**
**XT90 male**

A. Slot the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female** into its compartment in the **power housing**. The prong faces up.

B. Seat the positive line in the positive cup of the **XT90 male**, and the negative line in the negative cup. Match the polarity marks on the connector. Add solder if you want each line held in its cup.

C. Slot the female XT90 and the **XT90 male** into the **power housing**, on the end opposite the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**.

## 15. Attaching the motor wires to the ESCs

<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**
<img src="catalog/esc-casing.png" alt="ESC casing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **ESC casing**
<img src="catalog/esc-casing.png" alt="ESC casing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **ESC casing**
**ESC**
**Flywheel motor**

A. Take each **Flywheel motor**'s three wires, already routed around the pillar in the **power housing**, and bring that end to the three pins on top of the **ESC** in that **ESC casing**.

Swap any two of the three wires on an **ESC** to reverse that **Flywheel motor**. Either **ESC** may need this. The **flywheel**s must spin in opposite directions, and both must spin inward, so the ammunition passes through the barrel.

## 16. Attaching the ESC signal wires to the Molex

**ESC**
**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**

A. Take the lead from slot 5 of the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female** and the white wire from one **ESC**. Twist them together and solder the joint.

B. Take the lead from slot 6 of the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female** and the white wire from the other **ESC**. Twist them together and solder the joint.

Either white wire can go to either slot. Each white wire goes to one of slot 5 and slot 6.

## 17. Installing the power housing ceiling

<img src="catalog/ph-ceiling-pye-connector.png" alt="PH ceiling / PYE connector" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **PH ceiling / PYE connector**
<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**

A. Align the **PH ceiling / PYE connector** on top of the **power housing** so its three holes line up with the three pillars. Secure it with three 8 mm **M3 bolts**. Each bolt passes through the **PH ceiling / PYE connector** and threads into the insert in a pillar.

## 18. Installing the electrical components on the barrel middle

<img src="catalog/barrel-middle.png" alt="barrel middle" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel middle**
<img src="catalog/lasenoid-base.png" alt="lasenoid base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **lasenoid base**
<img src="catalog/solenoid-rod-pin.png" alt="solenoid / rod pin" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **solenoid / rod pin**
<img src="catalog/actuation-rod.png" alt="actuation rod" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **actuation rod**
<img src="catalog/laser-carriage.png" alt="laser carriage" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **laser carriage**
<img src="catalog/pinion-gear.png" alt="pinion gear" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **pinion gear**
**N20 motor**
**LED stick**
**Solenoid**
**laser**
**JST-PH 2.0 mm, 2-pin**
**JST-PH 2.0 mm, 3-pin**

A. Place the **LED stick** into its cutout in the **barrel middle**. Route the power, ground, and signal lines through the cutout channels.

B. Press the **pinion gear** onto the shaft of the cone **N20 motor**, then slot the motor into the **barrel middle**. Route its two leads through the wire channels.

C. Slot the **Solenoid** into the **lasenoid base**. Pin the solenoid end to the **actuation rod** with the **solenoid / rod pin**. The other end of the **actuation rod** attaches to the **laser carriage**. Seat the **laser** in the **laser carriage**, and slot the carriage into the **lasenoid base**. Seat that assembly in its compartment in the **barrel middle**.

D. Solder each component's leads to a **JST-PH 2.0 mm** pigtail. The cone **N20 motor**, the **laser**, and the **Solenoid** each take a 2-pin. The **LED stick** takes a 3-pin: pin 1 power, pin 2 signal, pin 3 ground.

These pigtails mate to the right-angle **JST-PH 2.0 mm** headers on the logic board.

## 19. Installing the logic board

<img src="catalog/barrel-middle.png" alt="barrel middle" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel middle**
**logic board**
**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 male**

A. Solder the leads from pins 1, 4, 5, and 6 of the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 male** to the same-numbered pads on the **logic board**.

- Pin 1 is power.
- Pin 4 is ground.
- Pin 5 is ESC 1.
- Pin 6 is ESC 2.

Pins 2 and 3 stay empty.

B. Place the **logic board** on the back of the **barrel middle**. Align the hole in the board with the heat-set insert in the **barrel middle**.

C. Secure the **logic board** to the **barrel middle** with one 6 mm **M3 bolt**. The bolt passes through the **logic board** and threads into the insert.

## 20. Installing the dial N20

<img src="catalog/flywheel-carriage.png" alt="flywheel carriage" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel carriage**
<img src="catalog/barrel-middle.png" alt="barrel middle" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel middle**
**N20 motor**
**LED stick**
**logic board**
**JST-PH 2.0 mm, 2-pin**

A. Take the dial **N20 motor**. Solder a **JST-PH 2.0 mm, 2-pin** onto its leads. The lead, from the motor to the connector, is at least 120 mm long.

B. Slot the **N20 motor** into the top of the **flywheel carriage**.

C. Route the lead under the top of the **flywheel carriage**, through the top of the **barrel middle**, under the **LED stick**, and through the wire channels up to the **logic board**. Plug the connector into J2, the dial header.

## 21. Installing the magazine mount

<img src="catalog/magazine-mount.png" alt="magazine mount" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **magazine mount**
<img src="catalog/barrel-middle.png" alt="barrel middle" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel middle**
<img src="catalog/flywheel-carriage.png" alt="flywheel carriage" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel carriage**
<img src="catalog/power-housing.png" alt="power housing" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **power housing**
**logic board**
**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 male**
**Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female**
**N20 motor**

A. Plug the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 male** from the **logic board** into the **Micro-Fit 3.0 1×6 female** in the **power housing**. Seat the **barrel middle** against the **flywheel carriage** so both ends sit flush.

B. Place the **magazine mount** on top of the **barrel middle** and the **flywheel carriage**. Route the dial **N20 motor** wires through the underside of the **magazine mount**.

C. Secure the **magazine mount** with three 6 mm **M3 bolts**. Two pass through the **magazine mount** and thread into the **barrel middle**. One passes through the **magazine mount** and threads into the **flywheel carriage**.

## 22. Preparing and installing the drum

<img src="catalog/drum-cam.png" alt="drum cam" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **drum cam**
<img src="catalog/drum-main.png" alt="drum main" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **drum main**
<img src="catalog/drum-motor-shaft.png" alt="drum motor shaft" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **drum motor shaft**
<img src="catalog/flywheel-base.png" alt="flywheel base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel base**
**N20 motor**

A. Slot the **drum cam** into the **drum main**. The male prongs on the **drum cam** seat in the female counterparts on the **drum main**.

B. Attach the **drum motor shaft** onto the dial **N20 motor**.

C. Install the assembled drum onto the **drum motor shaft**. The drum's guide rails sit flush between the guide on the **flywheel base**.

## 23. Assembling and installing the plunger

<img src="catalog/plunger-mount.png" alt="plunger mount" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **plunger mount**
<img src="catalog/plunger.png" alt="plunger" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **plunger**
<img src="catalog/flywheel-base.png" alt="flywheel base" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel base**
<img src="catalog/drum-main.png" alt="drum main" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **drum main**
**Plunger spring**

A. Insert the **plunger** and the **Plunger spring** into the **plunger mount**. The **plunger** faces the **drum main**.

B. Secure the **plunger mount** to the **flywheel base** with one 35 mm **M3 bolt**. The bolt passes through the **plunger mount** and threads into the insert in the **flywheel base**. Align the mount so the **plunger** faces the drum.

Give the **drum main** a manual test spin. From the rear, looking toward the front of the barrel, it turns clockwise.

## 24. Preparing and installing the barrel panels

<img src="catalog/barrel-base-l-panel.png" alt="barrel base L panel" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel base L panel**
<img src="catalog/barrel-base-r-panel.png" alt="barrel base R panel" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel base R panel**
<img src="catalog/name-lasenoid-panel.png" alt="name & lasenoid panel" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **name & lasenoid panel**
<img src="catalog/led-cone-n20-motor-panel.png" alt="led & cone n20 motor panel" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **led & cone n20 motor panel**
<img src="catalog/flywheel-carriage.png" alt="flywheel carriage" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **flywheel carriage**
<img src="catalog/barrel-middle.png" alt="barrel middle" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel middle**
**magnet**

A. Press one **magnet** into the **barrel base L panel**, and one **magnet** into the **barrel base R panel**. Orient each magnet so it attracts the counterpart in the **flywheel carriage**.

B. Press two **magnets** into the **name & lasenoid panel**. Orient each magnet so it attracts its counterpart in the **barrel middle**.

C. Press two **magnets** into the **led & cone n20 motor panel**. Orient each magnet so it attracts its counterpart in the **barrel middle**.

D. Seat the four panels on their magnets. The **barrel base L panel** and the **barrel base R panel** go on the **flywheel carriage**. The **name & lasenoid panel** and the **led & cone n20 motor panel** go on the **barrel middle**.

<img src="catalog/barrel-middle.png" alt="barrel middle" width="48" style="width: 48px; max-width: 48px; height: auto; vertical-align: middle;"> **barrel middle**
**Ring bearing**

A. Press the **cone** onto the top of the **barrel middle**, where the **Ring bearing** sits.
