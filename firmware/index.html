FLYWHEELS

Two speed controllers

GPIO1 is flywheel 1 (Molex pin 5). GPIO2 is flywheel 2 (Molex pin 6). Arm at idle, then pick a throttle. The cap limits that command. 100% means the cap, not full stick. Coast eases a PWM signal down over one second, then that wheel disarms. DShot stops at once. Arm again before the next run. Clamp the barrel before either wheel turns.

INDEXER

DIAL

GPIO4 and GPIO5 drive the drum motor on J2. Any run is full 6 V for the time you set. Reverse the direction if the drum turns the wrong way. Coast releases the motor. Brake holds it.

—

MUZZLE

Cone

GPIO6 and GPIO7 drive the cone motor on J3. Same full-voltage run as the indexer. A turning cone is the ready cue.

—

LASENOID

Laser

GPIO8 switches the laser on J5. Do not look into the beam or aim it at anyone. Pulse turns it off by itself. Stay on holds until Laser off or E-stop.

—

LASENOID

Solenoid

GPIO9 pulses the solenoid on J6. It always turns off by itself. The longest pulse is 8 seconds. E-stop ends one early.

—

BARREL MIDDLE

LED stick

GPIO10 is the data line on J4. The stick on this barrel is 12 pixels. Light one pixel to walk the chain. A dark tail means the data stops at the last lit pixel.
